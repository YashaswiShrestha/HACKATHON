VISTA turns your smartphone into an intuitive visual guide. Built specifically for accessibility, VISTA listens to your voice and looks through your camera to help you navigate everyday moments. Whether you need to locate your water bottle, read a label on a shelf, or get a quick description of a new room, VISTA delivers fast, conversational audio answers—no clutter, no complex menus, just clear visual understanding.
Here is the complete user guide and list of commands for using VISTA.

📱 How to Set Up & Start
Open VISTA: Launch the app in your browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge recommended).

Grant Permissions: Allow Camera and Microphone access when prompted.

Point Your Phone: Hold your device steadily, facing the direction or object you want to analyze.

🎙️️ Core Feature Commands
1. DESCRIBE (General Scene Overview)
What it does: Captures the live camera frame and speaks a quick, natural summary of your surroundings—prioritizing doors, people, obstacles, furniture, and room layout.

How to trigger:

Touch: Tap the large DESCRIBE button on the top half of the screen.

Keyboard: Press D.

2. ASK (Visual Q&A)
What it does: Lets you ask any custom question about what the camera currently sees and gives you a direct, concise spoken answer.

How to trigger:

Touch: Tap the large ASK button (or the Microphone button), wait for the chime/prompt, and speak your question.

Keyboard: Press A.

Example Voice Commands to Speak:

"What color is this shirt?"

"Is there a chair nearby?"

"Is the door open or closed?"

"What is on the table in front of me?"

"Are there any people standing near me?"

3. READ (Text-to-Speech)
What it does: Scans for visible text in front of the camera (signs, labels, documents, menus) and reads it out loud. For long text, it summarizes the key info first.

How to trigger:

Touch: Tap the large READ button.

Keyboard: Press R.

4. FIND (Object Locator)
What it does: Scans the scene for a specific item you name and gives you its simple spatial location (e.g., "Slightly to the left on the desk").

How to trigger:

Touch: Tap the large FIND button, then speak the item you are looking for.

Keyboard: Press F.

Example Voice Commands to Speak:

"Find my water bottle."

"Find the red backpack."

"Find the doorway."

"Find my keys."
